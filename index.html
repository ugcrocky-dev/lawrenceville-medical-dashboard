Georgia legal reality (do this first)

In Georgia, a PA cannot own a medical practice or med spa. Clinical care must sit in a physician-owned entity, with a board-approved supervising physician and PA job description. Your best path: you own a Management Services Organization (MSO) for marketing, ops, and software; a physician owns the clinical PC; your wife practices under that physician. As of May 2026, GCMB tightened “rent-a-supervisor” and IV/med-spa oversight — get a healthcare attorney before treating patients.
